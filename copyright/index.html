Copyright and reuse

You may reuse this work, with credit.

The writing, books, diagrams and videos here are under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. The code is under the MIT licence.

Last reviewed 15 November 2026.

The short version

  • Writing, books, PDFs, diagrams and videos: CC BY 4.0. You may copy, share, translate and adapt them, including for commercial use, as long as you credit me, link to the source and say if you changed anything.
  • Code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python scripts and lab code): the MIT licence.
  • Not covered: my photo, third-party logos and marks, and text quoted from standards or other authors. Those belong to their owners.

How to credit

Name me, link to the page you used, and link to the licence. For example:

“Machine identities, tested in public” by Roshan Trivedi, roshantrivedi.co.in/nhi-lab/, CC BY 4.0.

If you changed it, add “adapted” or say what you changed. If you quote a result from a lab, please quote the case or round it comes from, so readers can check it.

What credit does not mean

Crediting me does not mean I endorse you, your product or your version. Please do not suggest that I do. The data in the labs and case studies is made up, and the standards mappings are my own reading, not legal advice or a certification.

If you find a copy without credit

Thank you for telling me. Use the contact options on the contact page and send the link. I first ask the site owner to add the credit or take the copy down, and only then go to the host.
