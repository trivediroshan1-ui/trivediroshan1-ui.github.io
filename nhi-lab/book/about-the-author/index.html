Machine identities, tested in public · Author

About the author

Roshan Trivedi, Bengaluru.

I make secure access something people can actually use. I have spent fourteen years in identity and privileged access, and I now own an enterprise credential-management platform and its privileged access product. In practice that means architecture, service design, standards and the roadmap, and just as much the work of getting teams to adopt it and being able to show an auditor how it works.

Most of my thinking lately goes into one question: what happens to identity when the user is a service account, a pipeline or an AI agent? This lab is one answer, tested in public, with the mistakes left in.

The case studies and labs on my site are anonymised. They leave out employers, clients and internal data on purpose.
