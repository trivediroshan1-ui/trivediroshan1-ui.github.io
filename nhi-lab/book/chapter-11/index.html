Step 7: Retire and respond

Remove what is unused, cut access fast after a breach.

What nobody uses should go, in stages, and access to a breached vendor should end quickly. This step covers retired applications, vendor sessions, AI-tool grants, a breached vendor and automations that outlive a test.

What the cases in this step showed

  • After an application was retired, all 3 of its identities still worked 90 days later.
  • A vendor session left no record and no end. A broker that recorded it closed it at 4 hours.
  • Five automations outlived a test. A sweep disabled 4 of them.

A question to ask

When did you last switch off something nobody could name an owner for?

8 cases follow. All guesses in this step matched. Controls nothing noticed: N-R14-23, C-R16-C04, N-R14-16.

Diagram for case S-R17-01: see it on the lab page.

GUESSMatched in both runs (control off, control on).

N-R14-23 Vendor breached, OAuth refresh token still valid

Step 7: Retire and respond · Run 1 · Gap cases from 2026 incidents · OWASP NHI3 · Guess matched · Control masked · Limit recorded

An employee linked an AI office app with "allow all" 20 days ago. The vendor is then breached. The attacker replays the refresh token from their own network.

Diagram for case N-R14-23: see it on the lab page.

GUESSMatched in both runs (control off, control on).
MEASUREDWithout: grant accepted yes, replay min30 yes, replay min90 yes. With: grant accepted no, replay min30 no, replay min90 no.
EXTRA SCENARIORevoke only (app already granted). Result matched my guess.
EXTRA SCENARIORevoke off, allowlist off. Result matched my guess.
TAKE-AWAYLetting every app in lets a breached vendor's token in. An allowlist refused the grant. An allowlisted app that is breached still works until you revoke it, and revoking ended replays only after 60 minutes.

C-R16-C04 The contractor who never left

Step 7: Retire and respond · Run 2 · Chain tests · Guess matched · Control masked

Chain of links: no end date, contract ends in agency records, account stays, phished credential used.

Diagram for case C-R16-C04: see it on the lab page.

GUESSMatched in both runs (control off, control on).
MEASUREDWithout controls: the attacker reaches the last link. With all controls: stopped at link 3. Each control alone: Expire at the end date stops it at link 3; Sponsor review does not stop it. Best single control: Expire at the end date.
EXTRA SCENARIOAttack 120 days after contract end, sponsor review runs at 90. Result matched my guess.
TAKE-AWAYExpiring the account at the end date stops the chain at link 3. A sponsor review alone leaves a window of up to 90 days. If the attack comes 120 days after the contract ends, the review does stop it.

N-R14-16 Retire before you rotate

Step 7: Retire and respond · Run 4 · Own and retire · OWASP NHI1 · Guess matched · Control masked · Limit recorded

30 identities are due for rotation. 12 have not been used for 90 days or more. 1 of those 12 is used by a quarterly job.

Diagram for case N-R14-16: see it on the lab page.

GUESSMatched in both runs (control off, control on).
MEASUREDWithout: disabled 0, rotated 30, outages 0, permanent loss 0. With: disabled 12, rotated 18, outages 1, permanent loss 0.
EXTRA SCENARIOHold off. Result matched my guess.
EXTRA SCENARIOUse check off. Result matched my guess.
TAKE-AWAYThe use check disabled 12 of 30 identities, rotated the other 18, and 1 outage followed. The 30 day hold meant nothing was lost for good. Without the hold, that identity would have been lost permanently. Use logs that miss an older sign-in route make a used identity look idle.
