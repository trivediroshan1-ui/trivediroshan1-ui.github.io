Machine identities, tested in public · Chapter 5 · Framework step 1 of 7

Step 1: Find

List every machine identity and where it lives.

You cannot own or retire what you cannot see. This step builds one list from every place identities live: the directory, the cloud, the CI settings, the SaaS apps, the repositories and the AI tools people have connected.

What the cases in this step showed

  • A directory-only scan found 12 of 27 identities. Merging three sources found all 27.
  • Nine of ten targets were recorded, and nobody knew about the tenth until a coverage check compared the two lists.
  • Keys for AI services turned up in six repositories. A scan found five of the six.

A question to ask

Where does your list come from, and which source would you add first?

6 cases follow. Guesses that were wrong: N-R14-07, S-R17-08. Controls nothing noticed: S-R17-06.

N-R14-07 One identity, three names

Step 1: Find · Run 2 · Discover · Guess wrong · Limit recorded

The same service account appears as `svc-billing` in the directory, `billing-svc@kaveri` in the cloud and `BILLING_SVC` in the vault. 30 identities, 9 of them appear under two or three names.

Diagram for case N-R14-07: see it on the lab page.

GUESSWrong. I guessed 51 entries without the control and 30 with it. The true counts are 43 and 29. Nine identities with 2 or 3 names each cannot make 51. The rule also merged two real accounts.
MEASUREDWithout: count 43, split findings yes, wrong merges 0. With: count 29, split findings no, wrong merges 1.
TAKE-AWAYOne account with three names splits findings: 43 entries for 30 identities. Matching on owner and normalised name gave 29, one short, because it merged two real accounts. My own arithmetic in the guess was wrong too.

S-R17-08 An agent built in a low code tool and never registered

Step 1: Find · Run 4 · Shadow AI · Guess wrong · Limit recorded

6 agents are built by business users. 2 are registered. 4 are not and hold live credentials.

Diagram for case S-R17-08: see it on the lab page.

GUESSWrong. I guessed 4 agents would hold credentials at day 10 with the issue rule off, and that the 30 day expiry would go unnoticed. The scan had already flagged them and 3 owners had registered, so 1 agent holds credentials. The expiry is what disables the last one.
MEASUREDWithout: found 0, flagged 0, registered after request 0, disabled 0, unregistered with creds day10 4, unregistered with creds day31 4. With: found 6, flagged 4, registered after request 3, disabled 1, unregistered with creds day10 0, unregistered with creds day31 0.
EXTRA SCENARIOScan off. Result matched my guess.
EXTRA SCENARIOIssue rule off. Result differs from my guess: {'unregistered_with_creds_day10': [4, 1]}.
TAKE-AWAYThe weekly scan found all 6 agents and flagged the 4 unregistered ones. Three owners registered, so 1 agent stayed unregistered, and the 30 day expiry is what disabled it. An agent under a personal account is not in the tenant list the scan reads.
