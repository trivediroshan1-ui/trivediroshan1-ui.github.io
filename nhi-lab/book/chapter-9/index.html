Step 5: Rotate and expire

Change on a schedule, consumers first, old value proven dead.

Changing a secret is easy. Changing it without an outage, and proving the old value is dead, is where it goes wrong. This step covers rotation order, expiry, failed jobs, just-in-time windows and ranking what to fix first.

What the cases in this step showed

  • Rotating before updating the application made it fail at the next connection.
  • Five rotation tickets were closed while 2 old secrets still worked.
  • Rotating 200 secrets caused 12 outages. Consumers first and a dry run caused none.

A question to ask

After your last rotation, did anyone test that the old secret was refused?

10 cases follow. All guesses in this step matched.
