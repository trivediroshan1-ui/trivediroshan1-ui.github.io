Step 3: Limit

Least privilege and a front door that refuses the wrong caller.

A credential should reach as little as the job needs, and the door in front of it should refuse the wrong caller. This step covers least privilege, trust between workloads, approvals, multi-factor sign-in and the old doors that stay open next to a new vault.

What the cases in this step showed

  • A low-value workload got a high-value role until trust was bound to one workload.
  • Of 40 admin group members, 8 did privileged work. Pruning removed 32.
  • In two chain tests the control that blocked the path was not the one that only reported it.

A question to ask

Does anything still let a person or a tool walk around your vault?

18 cases follow. Guesses that were wrong: S-R17-09. Controls nothing noticed: P-R15-16, C-R16-C06, N-R18-02, N-R14-12, P-R15-04, P-R15-23, S-R17-07.
