The result

66rows run in this lab: 60 technical rows and 6 chain tests
35rows tested in an earlier lab, not re-run here
6rows partly tested, with the missing part stated
60rows not run: 46 technical rows and 14 documented chains

The 147 technical rows are the non-human identity, shadow AI and privileged access rows plus six gap rows. The 20 chains and the 26 business use cases make up the rest. Business use cases are not tested directly. Each one is rolled up from the technical rows it links to: 10 are supported by lab cases, 11 partly, 3 have no case behind them and 2 are not tested here (a reporting use, and joiners and movers, which the AI governance lab tested).

The lab itself has 67 cases. Sixty of them map to catalogue rows, six are chain tests, and N-R18-01 (round 18, insecure authentication) extends rows N34 and N36 without being a row of its own.

Download the register as CSV (193 rows).

What each status means

  • Run in this lab. A case exists, with guesses written and hashed first and a result recorded.
  • Tested in an earlier lab. The AI governance lab or the MCP lab tested it. I list their case ids and did not run them again.
  • Partly tested. Part of the row was tested. The page says which part was not.
  • Not run. No case exists. The reason is one of five kinds, below.
