Cover letter

A letter for hiring managers

Who I am, what I have done and the kind of role I am looking for.

Roshan TrivediBengaluru, India · linkedin.com/in/roshan-trivedi-ciam · roshantrivedi.co.in

Dear hiring manager,

I am looking for a senior identity and privileged access role where about half the job is leading people and half is designing the system. I currently own a credential management platform and the privileged access product portfolio around it, and I have spent 14+ years in IAM and PAM across India, the UK and Australia.

What I have done

The work I am proudest of is the unglamorous kind. A multi-year PAM strategy and Zero Standing Privilege roadmap that turned a multi-vendor proof of value into a phased target architecture. A secrets inventory that showed where secrets were stale or had no clear owner. Identity controls mapped to NIST CSF 2.0 and NIST SP 800-53, so an auditor can see who owns each control and what the evidence is.

Before that I led PAM Center of Excellence work at Optiv. At Deloitte in Sydney I led the delivery team for a CyberArk Privilege Cloud rollout and a PAM current-state assessment and roadmap for a major Australian bank.

How I work

I have led and mentored delivery teams since 2017. I write the standard, the service design and the runbook, and I stay close enough to the platform to know when a plan will not survive a real mix of Windows, Linux, network and ERP systems. I try to give application owners a clear path onto the platform.

Proof you can check

On my own time I test whether identity and AI controls hold when you try to break them. I write my guess down before the code runs, then keep the results where I was wrong. That is 164 AI governance cases, 48 machine identity cases and 210 checks on an approval gateway for AI agents. Four of my machine identity guesses were wrong, and the page says so. There are also 22 case studies, all written without employer or client data.

What I am looking for

Roles such as Head of IAM, PAM lead, platform owner or principal architect, at product companies, large enterprises, security vendors and service firms. I am based in Bengaluru and open to remote work.

If this sounds like a fit, message me on LinkedIn. I reply there. My résumé has the full list of roles and certifications, each linking to the issuer’s own record.

Sincerely,Roshan TrivediBengaluru, India
