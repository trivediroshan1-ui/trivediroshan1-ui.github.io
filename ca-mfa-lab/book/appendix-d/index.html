Cloud Security Alliance, Defining Non-Human Identity, 22 July 2026
https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/defining-non-human-identity		Industry researchNames six risk areas for all non-human identities, including missing owners, missing decommissioning triggers and access that grows through delegation. For agents it advises a human sponsor for each agent, sub-agents with minimal short-lived access limited to the human’s own permissions, and logs that record the delegator, the agent and the intent.G03 G04 G05 G10
