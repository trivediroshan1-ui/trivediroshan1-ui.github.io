Who signs in? · What I would check first

Conclusion

If I owned sign-in for an organisation tomorrow, I would not start with a new MFA method. I would start with these:

  1. Which sign-ins does no rule cover? Look for the default, the exceptions and the legacy paths.
  2. Is account recovery at least as strong as sign-in, including the help desk?
  3. Does a password reset end refresh tokens and sessions, everywhere?
  4. Does every privileged action check fresh MFA on the server, for keys and service tokens too?
  5. Does every agent have a named owner, an expiry tied to its task, and a resource that checks the human's own rights?
  6. When access is revoked, how long does each kind of token keep working?

This lab cannot tell you how your product behaves. It can tell you which questions to ask, and what the answer depends on. The six hand tests and the exception form are there so people with real tenants can tell me where I am wrong.
