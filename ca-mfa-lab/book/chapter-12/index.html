Who signs in? · One assumption at a time

What changes the answer

Every model rests on assumptions I chose: how fast an attacker replays a token, how often people read a prompt, how long a session lasts. For Runs 5 and 6 I changed one assumption at a time and re-ran. This table lists every change and what it did.

Assumption changedCases that flippedWhat happened
p_no_read 0.02 (people almost always read)W10, W11W10: in out in out out became in out out out out; W11: in out in out out became in out out out out

Read the flips as a list of what to check in your own setup. If your people almost always read a confirmation prompt, approval does real work. If your continuous evaluation only watches the network, an attacker who looks local gets past it. If your secret scanner does not read build logs, it will not find a secret leaked in one.
