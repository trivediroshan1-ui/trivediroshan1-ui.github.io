Research and development: where vendors and standards are going

I searched once, in November 2026, for what identity vendors and standards bodies are building for non-human identity, AI agents, MFA and SSO. Most of what came back is vendor material or a write-up of it, so each row says what kind of source it is. Product claims are the vendor's.
