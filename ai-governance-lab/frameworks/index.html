Where the controls sit in ISO 27001, NIST 800-53, PCI DSS and HIPAA

The first sixteen controls were mapped to AI frameworks (ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, the EU AI Act and Indian guidance), and that table is on the run page. Auditors in finance and healthcare also ask about the older standards, so this table shows the same controls against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A, NIST SP 800-53, PCI DSS 4.0 and HIPAA, plus seven new controls (C17 to C23) for the round-five cases. A dash means I did not map it.

This is my own reading, not legal advice and not a certification. Standards change and numbering differs between versions, so check each reference against your own copy before you rely on it. For the Indian rules on the new controls: DPDP Act section 8 (safeguards, processors) and section 16 (transfers), the RBI master direction on outsourcing of IT services, and the CERT-In directions on logs.
