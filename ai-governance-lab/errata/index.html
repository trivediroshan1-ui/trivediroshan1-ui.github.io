Errata for the AI governance book and addendum

Three rounds changed after the PDFs were made. This page says which numbers to read instead, page by page.

Last reviewed 15 November 2026

The short version

  • On 25 October 2026 I re-counted rounds 6 to 12 with the stricter rule from round 13. Rounds 6, 7 and 9 dropped. The book, the addendum and the round videos were made before that, so they still show the older numbers.
  • The numbers printed in the PDFs have not been corrected. They are the numbers as first published, and this page is the fix.
  • Nothing else moved. Every “as guessed” result, every guess hash and rounds 8, 10, 11, 12 and 13 are as printed. The round five PDF is not affected, because round five has no control-off checks.

What to read instead

RoundPrintedReadNot noticed
612 of 1211 of 12T5: the agent’s own sponsor may approve
714 of 1412 of 14H4: the same person may give both approvals. H8: the signed policy is not required
912 of 12 (addendum page 46 says 14 of 15)13 of 15Q5, twice: the gateway check for unknown actions is switched off. The 15 includes three stronger checks added later

The stricter rule says a case notices a missing control only if it passes normally and then gives a different result that is not an error. In these three rounds the case crashed with the control off. A crash shows the code broke. It does not show the check caught the problem.

Q5 is a different case. With the gateway check off, the executor still refuses the request, so nothing changes. That is a second layer doing its job, and it means Q5 cannot show the gateway check matters on its own.

The full account, with the pull request and the dates, is in What I got wrong.
