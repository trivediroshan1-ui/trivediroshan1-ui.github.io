AI governance lab

Try it yourself

Run the same plain filters I tested, on your own made-up text, in your browser. Then test your own assistant against the same nine requests I used.

This page is the hands-on part of the write-up. The three filters are ported from the Python lab, and I checked the port against the original on more than 2,000 texts: the masked output, the findings and the injection flag were identical every time. Nothing you type leaves your device. If you find a text that fools a filter, I would like to know.

What to expect

  • The filters are deliberately plain: regular expressions, the Aadhaar and card checksums, and a short list of injection phrases. They are not a product.
  • Every number on this page is made up and generated in your browser. The numbers follow real formats on purpose, so a filter cannot tell them from real ones.
  • v3 was written to fix what v2 missed on the lab’s own samples, so its high scores here are not a detection rate.
  • The assistant test covers one policy and nine requests. A pass is a data point, not a guarantee.
