AI governance lab

The Mac record

Every command I ran on my MacBook Air, what it printed, and every result file it produced, with a SHA-256 for each. Three sessions over two days. The mistakes are in here too.

I ran the lab on my own Mac so that the results do not depend only on a sandbox that I built and control. This page is the plain record of that. The commands are in the order I typed them. Next to each one is what came back. At the bottom are the three terminal sessions as they were, unedited. Every name, ID number and password in the output is made-up test data about an invented lender, Kaveri Finance.

Two things to keep in mind. First, I picked the Mac runs to confirm the sandbox, so the cases and the guesses were written before the Mac ran them, and the Mac result files are the ones I pushed straight from the terminal. Second, a Mac is one machine. It shows the code does not depend on my sandbox. It does not show the lab is right about real systems.
