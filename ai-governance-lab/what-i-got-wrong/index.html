What I got wrong

Every mistake I found in my own lab, with the date and what changed. The wrong files stay in the repository, so you can check both.

25 Oct 2026: a crash counted as noticing (rounds 6 to 12)

For every control, I switch it off in a copy of the code and check whether a case notices. Round 13 brought in a stricter rule: a case notices only if it passes normally and then gives a different result that is not an error. Rounds 6 to 12 still used the older rule, where a crash also counted as noticing.

An automated review comment on an old pull request pointed at it. I applied the round 13 rule to rounds 6 to 12 and ran them again on my MacBook Air.

What it means

  • T5, H4 and H8 are weak tests. With the control off, the case crashed instead of showing the bad approval going through. A crash shows the code broke, not that the check caught the problem. I will rewrite these three so the failure is visible.
  • Q5 is a second layer, not a broken test. With the gateway check off, the executor still blocks the unknown action, so no case sees a change. That is defence in depth. It also means Q5 cannot show that the gateway check matters on its own.
  • What did not change: rounds 8, 10, 11 and 12, every "as guessed" result, and every guesses hash.

The books, the PDF of rounds five to thirteen and the round videos were made before this re-count and show the older numbers. The Mac record shows what the Mac printed at the time and is not edited. The change and the new result files are in pull request 15.

Round 13: my checker said "5 of 5 noticed" while every case had errored

It counted an error as noticing a change. I fixed it so an error never counts, and kept the wrong file. This is the rule that, on 25 Oct, I found I had not applied to rounds 6 to 12.

Rounds 1 to 4: the 100% filter score was tuned

Three versions of a plain text filter caught 20, 42 and 45 of 53 attack variants. On 10 items written after the fix, it caught 4. The 100% on the 200-sample set only showed that I had tuned it to that set.

First runs that did not match, and why

  • Round 5: one test crashed on the first run. My test, fixed.
  • Round 7: first run 13 of 14 control-off checks. One weak test, rebuilt.
  • Round 9: first run 11 of 12 as guessed. A bug in my masking, fixed.
  • Round 11: first run 11 of 12 as guessed. My own test, fixed.
  • Mac runs broke on package versions (numpy 2 with the torch build I used).

Guesses that may be too easy

I wrote both the guesses and the code. When a round comes out exactly as I guessed, I read that as a warning that my guesses were easy, not as good news about the controls. If you see a weak test, propose a case.
