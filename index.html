01Perspective

Identity is the control plane.

I work where platform strategy, security architecture, product ownership and audit evidence meet.

I translate difficult identity-security problems into products people can adopt, teams can operate and leaders can govern.

My current focus spans credential-management platform ownership, PAM product leadership and IAM control evaluation. The work includes product roadmaps, architecture decisions, use-case analysis, proof-of-concept leadership, migrations and audit mapping.

I also analyze non-human identity, MCP and agentic-AI security, using AI as a co-worker while keeping privacy, source validation and human accountability explicit.

Public by design.Every portfolio case study is intentionally anonymized and excludes employer, client and internal operational data. My résumé (linked above) is the deliberate exception — it includes full employment history for verification.
