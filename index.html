New · open source · live

MCP Human Approval Gateway

A working lab for one question: when an AI agent asks to use a tool, who decides, and what exactly was approved? Policy decides, a person who is not the requester approves, and the approval is tied to a hash of that exact request. It works once and it expires. If a response is lost, the call stays unresolved until a reviewer settles it.

  • Speaks MCP revision 2026-07-28, with 2025-11-25 compatibility
  • 203 automated tests, hash-chained audit log
  • Tools are synthetic. Tried by hand with the official MCP Inspector v2.9.0 (MCP 2025-11-25): approval, single use and an over-scope denial all behaved. No other client and no real environment yet.
