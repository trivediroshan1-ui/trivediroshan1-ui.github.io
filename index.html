I'm a cybersecurity professional specialising in Identity & Access Management (IAM) and Privileged Access Management (PAM). Day to day, I work as a PAM Product Owner — building and running the platforms that govern privileged access, enforce least privilege, and make every sensitive action accountable.

My conviction: security should be architectural, not procedural. Controls built into the system — zero standing privileges, just-in-time access, cryptographic enforcement, continuous monitoring — hold. Policies written in a document, enforced by humans under pressure, do not.

Beyond my day job, I research and publish independent, open, public-interest proposals for problems that matter at national scale. Most recently: a zero-trust reference architecture to secure India's national examination system — from AI-assisted paper creation to post-exam destruction.
