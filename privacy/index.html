Privacy

What this site collects, and why.

Very little. No cookies, no ad trackers, no social media embeds. Here is all of it, in plain words.

Last reviewed 15 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Roshan Trivedi, Bengaluru, India, runs this site on his own. For any privacy question, or to ask for your data to be deleted, use the contact options on the home page. I reply within 30 days.

If you subscribe to the newsletter

  • What: your email address, and the date you subscribed.
  • Why: to send you the newsletter, one or two emails a month. Nothing else. It is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • Who processes it: Buttondown, the newsletter service, which stores the list and sends the emails. Buttondown is based in the United States.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe. Then I delete your address.
  • Your choices: every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask me to delete your address, or ask what I hold about you.

When you read the site

  • Page counts: I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people read each page. It sets no cookies and does not build a profile of you. It records the page, the referring site, the browser type and the country.
  • Delivery: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Like any web server, they see your IP address to send you the page and may keep it in short-lived security logs. I do not see or keep it.
  • Theme setting: if you switch between dark and light, your browser remembers the choice on your own device. It is never sent to me.

The chat assistant and the labs

The chat box answers from a fixed set of answers that load with the page. What you type stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere. The in-browser labs, including the Python runner, also run entirely on your device. All lab data is made up.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and withdraw your consent by unsubscribing. If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you these rights. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR does. If you are not happy with my answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. A bigger change will also be mentioned in the newsletter.
