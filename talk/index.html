Roles I would talk about

I am happy to talk about identity and privileged-access roles at manager level and above in India, remote included. That means leadership roles (senior manager, associate director, head of IAM, PAM lead), product and platform ownership roles (credential management product owner or platform owner, PAM or IAM platform owner), and principal-level architecture roles (principal or identity architect, AI security lead). If you are a recruiter, a first message with the role, the team and the location lets me answer properly.
