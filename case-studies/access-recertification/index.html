Governance

Access Recertification & IGA Automation

Turning a manual quarterly access review into a governed process backed by usage evidence.

Credential Management Platform Owner & PAM Product Owner

The challenge

Quarterly access certification asked managers to approve long entitlement lists from memory, so recertification became a rubber stamp instead of an actual control, and dormant access routinely survived every cycle.

The approach

  • Connected entitlement data to actual usage logs, so every certification decision starts from evidence of whether the access was used, not just whether it was granted.
  • Added an advisory recommendation that pre-flags likely-dormant entitlements for revocation, leaving the reviewer to confirm or override rather than evaluate every line from a blank state.
  • Fed every certification decision back into the identity governance system of record, so revocations execute automatically instead of generating a second manual follow-up task.

Outcome

Cut the average certification review time while increasing the revocation rate for dormant entitlements, because reviewers were confirming evidenced recommendations instead of reconstructing usage from memory.
