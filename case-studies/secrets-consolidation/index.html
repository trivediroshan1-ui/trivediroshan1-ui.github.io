Secrets governance

Secrets Sprawl & Consolidation

Turning credentials scattered across code, CI/CD and config files into a single governed vault.

Credential Management Platform Owner & PAM Product Owner

The challenge

Secrets accumulated in source repositories, pipeline variables and configuration files faster than any team could track, so the real exposure was unknown until an incident forced a manual search.

The approach

  • Scanned repositories, pipelines and configuration stores to build a single inventory of where credentials actually lived, not where policy assumed they lived.
  • Added an advisory classifier that ranks each discovered secret by exposure and blast radius, so remediation starts with what matters instead of working through the list in discovery order.
  • Migrated ranked secrets into a managed vault with rotation and access logging, and blocked new hard-coded credentials at the pipeline stage instead of catching them after merge.

Outcome

Replaced an unknown volume of scattered credentials with a ranked, vaulted inventory, and cut new hard-coded secrets entering the codebase through a pipeline-stage control.
