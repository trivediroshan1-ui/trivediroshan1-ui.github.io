Product evaluation

PAM, NHI & Cloud Platform Evaluation

Comparing emerging and established platforms through use-case fit, controls and operating-model readiness.

Evaluation and PoC lead

The challenge

Fast-moving Non-Human Identity (NHI), cloud security and privileged-access markets make feature lists insufficient for durable product decisions.

The approach

  • Built scenario-led assessments around identity types, trust boundaries and lifecycle responsibilities.
  • Completed Delinea Secret Server and PRA proof-of-concept work focused on practical use cases.
  • Led StrongDM product analysis and operating-model planning alongside structured assessments of Clutch Security and Wiz.

Outcome

Created decision-ready analysis that connects technical capability to governance, integration and product ownership.
