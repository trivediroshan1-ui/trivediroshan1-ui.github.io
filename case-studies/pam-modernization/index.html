Privileged access

PAM Modernization & Zero Standing Privilege

Evolving privileged access from persistent entitlement toward verified, time-bound access.

Product and architecture lead

The challenge

Traditional privileged-access patterns can leave standing access, inconsistent session controls and operational friction between security and users.

The mechanism

The approach

  • Developed a capability view spanning vaulting, rotation, session governance and just-in-time authorization.
  • Evaluated Zero Standing Privilege patterns against user journeys, integration constraints and control objectives.
  • Balanced security outcomes with adoption, supportability and recovery requirements.

Outcome

Established a modernization direction that treats privileged access as a governed product journey, not only a vault deployment.
