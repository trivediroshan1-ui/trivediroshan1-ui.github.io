Break-Glass Emergency Access

Designing emergency access that stays usable under pressure without becoming a permanent bypass.

Credential Management Platform Owner & PAM Product Owner

The challenge

Emergency access existed to keep incident response moving, but without controls on when it could be invoked, break-glass credentials were also the easiest path around normal approval — and nobody could tell the difference after the fact.

The approach

  • Separated break-glass credentials from standing accounts entirely, so invoking them is a distinct, logged event rather than a quiet reuse of existing access.
  • Added an advisory check that flags break-glass usage matching normal-hours, repeat-user patterns for reviewer attention, instead of treating every invocation as equally urgent.
  • Required a post-use justification and automatic credential rotation after every invocation, closing the loop the same day rather than at the next audit cycle.

Outcome

Every break-glass invocation now produces a reviewed justification and a rotated credential within the same day, turning emergency access from an audit blind spot into an evidenced, bounded exception.
