Privileged access

Just-in-Time & Just-Enough-Access Elevation

Replacing standing administrative access with time-boxed, scope-limited elevation that closes automatically.

Credential Management Platform Owner & PAM Product Owner

The challenge

Administrative rights were granted as standing role membership, so access outlived the task that justified it and every audit relied on people remembering to revoke what they no longer needed.

The mechanism

The approach

  • Mapped every standing privileged role to the specific tasks it was actually used for, to size the smallest workable elevation window instead of a generic default.
  • Added an advisory step where a policy-aware assistant proposes scope and duration from the request context — the requester and approver still make the actual decision.
  • Set elevation to expire automatically and revoke on task completion, so access reverts to zero by default instead of relying on a follow-up ticket.

Outcome

Converted the majority of standing administrative role assignments into time-boxed elevation, cutting the population of accounts with always-on privileged access without adding an approval bottleneck.
