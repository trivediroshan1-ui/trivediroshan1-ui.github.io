Cloud identity

Cloud Entitlement Management (CIEM) at Scale

Finding the toxic entitlement combinations that individual cloud IAM rules miss.

Credential Management Platform Owner & PAM Product Owner

The challenge

Cloud IAM policies were reviewed one role or one permission at a time, so combinations of individually reasonable entitlements that together created an unintended privilege escalation path went undetected until they were exploited or found by chance.

The approach

  • Built a unified view of effective permissions across cloud accounts and providers, since the real entitlement a principal holds is the union of every policy attached to it, not any single policy in isolation.
  • Added an advisory reasoning pass over the entitlement graph that surfaces combinations — not just individual over-permissioned roles — that together allow privilege escalation or unintended data access.
  • Routed every flagged combination through the same human-approval gate used for standing privileged access changes, so remediation follows an existing accountable process instead of a one-off cleanup.

Outcome

Surfaced toxic entitlement combinations that per-policy review had missed, and closed them through the existing privileged-access approval process rather than a separate one-time remediation effort.
