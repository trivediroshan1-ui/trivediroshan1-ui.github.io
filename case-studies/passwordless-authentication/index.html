Authentication

Passwordless Authentication Rollout

Sequencing a passwordless migration by risk instead of rolling it out to everyone at once.

Credential Management Platform Owner & PAM Product Owner

The challenge

A blanket passwordless rollout risked locking out the users least prepared for it, while the accounts most worth protecting — the highest-risk users and devices — would migrate on the same generic timeline as everyone else.

The approach

  • Inventoried authentication methods and device posture across the user population before setting a migration order, instead of assuming uniform readiness.
  • Added an advisory risk score per user and device that sequences migration waves — highest-risk accounts move to phishing-resistant authentication first, not last.
  • Kept a fallback authentication path available during each wave and retired it only after adoption and support-ticket data confirmed the wave was stable.

Outcome

Migrated the highest-risk accounts to phishing-resistant authentication ahead of the general population, and reduced credential-based helpdesk tickets as each wave completed instead of concentrating support load at a single cutover date.
