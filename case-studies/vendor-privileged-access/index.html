Privileged access

Third-Party & Vendor Privileged Access Governance

Governing external vendor access with the same scrutiny as internal privileged accounts, not less.

Credential Management Platform Owner & PAM Product Owner

The challenge

Vendor and contractor access was provisioned through the fastest available path — shared accounts, standing VPN credentials or ad hoc exceptions — because the standard onboarding process assumed an internal employee.

The approach

  • Built a distinct onboarding path for third-party access, scoped to a specific vendor, engagement and system rather than a general-purpose account.
  • Added an advisory summary that translates a vendor's requested scope into plain language for the resource owner, since approval quality depends on the owner actually understanding what they're granting.
  • Tied every vendor credential to the engagement's end date by default, so offboarding an external party doesn't depend on someone remembering to file a ticket.

Outcome

Replaced shared, standing vendor credentials with engagement-scoped access that expires with the contract, and gave resource owners a plain-language basis for every approval instead of a technical scope string.
