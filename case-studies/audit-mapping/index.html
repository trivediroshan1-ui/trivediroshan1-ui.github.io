Governance

IAM Audit & Control-Mapping Framework

Translating control intent into platform responsibility, implementation evidence and sustainable ownership.

IAM control evaluation contributor

The challenge

Audit language and platform implementation often describe the same risk differently, leaving ambiguity around evidence and ownership.

The approach

  • Evaluated Identity and Access Management (IAM) control intent against platform capabilities and operational processes.
  • Mapped control objectives to owners, evidence types, dependencies and review considerations.
  • Identified where policy, technology and operating practice needed clearer alignment.

Outcome

Improved the traceability between IAM design decisions and the evidence needed to demonstrate control effectiveness.
