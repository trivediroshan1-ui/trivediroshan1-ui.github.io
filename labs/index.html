Open-source lab · October 2026

HumanGate: a person in the loop for AI agents

A small approval gateway for MCP tool calls. Policy decides the risk, a person who is not the requester approves, and the approval is tied to that exact request, works once and expires. I tested it 210 times across three rounds, with made-up tools and data.
